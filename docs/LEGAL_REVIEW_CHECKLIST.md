# Legal review checklist (Egyptian counsel)

The texts in `src/content/legal.ts` are **reviewable drafts**, not final and not a compliance certification. Each section marked `review: true` is listed here.

## Regulatory characterisation
- [ ] Confirm that an organizational, non-custodial Gam3ya record-keeping tool falls outside licensing under the **Central Bank and Banking System Law No. 194/2020** and FRA rules (no fund collection, transfer, guarantee, lending or investment).
- [ ] Confirm wording avoids implying banking/payment services (no "wallet", "transfer", "guarantee", "verified payment").

## Personal data — Law No. 151/2020 (PDPL) and its Executive Regulations
- [ ] Lawful basis & consent wording for account data and Gam3ya records.
- [ ] **Cross-border transfer:** hosting on Google Cloud / Firebase (EU regions) — licensing/permit needs from the Personal Data Protection Centre.
- [ ] Controller registration / licensing obligations and DPO requirement.
- [ ] Data-subject rights process and response deadlines; retention periods for financial history after account deactivation/deletion.
- [ ] Breach notification procedure and timelines.

## Terms
- [ ] Limitation of liability enforceability under Egyptian Civil Code.
- [ ] Governing law & jurisdiction clause.
- [ ] Acceptable-use clause (prohibition on public fund collection).
- [ ] Consumer Protection Law No. 181/2018 considerations.

## Subscriptions (future)
- [ ] Pricing transparency, explicit consent, renewal and cancellation terms; e-payment/e-invoicing obligations before any billing is activated.
- [ ] Confirm statement that accepting current Terms is **not** consent to future paid plans.
